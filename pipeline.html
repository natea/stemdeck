The pipeline

Any YouTube video in, sheet music and tab out

Paste a YouTube or SoundCloud link, or drop in an audio file. StemDeck separates it into six stems on your machine, sends each stem to the model that hears it best, and turns the notes into a score you can read while the song plays.

Worth knowing

Separation: one Demucs model, htdemucs_6s, makes all six stems. Lead and backing vocals come from an optional second split of the vocals with UVR Karaoke 2.

Rhythm: beat tracking runs on the drums stem. Your own edits to the beat grid win, and changing the grid re-snaps the notes without running a model again.

Stored versus built: only the raw notes per stem are saved. Quantised notes, fingering, MIDI, MusicXML and the alphaTab score are rebuilt each time you ask.

As played: the MIDI download can skip quantising, and the Visualizers always use the notes as played.

Defaults: each stem's model can be changed with STEMDECK_TRANSCRIBE_<STEM>.
