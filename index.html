Transcribe

Every stem, through the model that hears it best

Separated stems are close to the clean, single-instrument audio that transcription models do best on. StemDeck also knows where every beat and bar falls, which is what turns raw notes into something readable.

  • Vocals, bassCREPE pitch tracking, then a step that turns the pitch curve into notes
  • PianoByteDance high-resolution piano transcription
  • Guitar, otherYourMT3+, which handles several notes at once
  • DrumsYourMT3+, mapped to General MIDI percussion
  • RhythmSnapped to the song's own beats and downbeats, including your edits. The raw notes are kept, so editing the grid re-snaps without running a model again.
  • DownloadsMIDI as played, MIDI on the beat grid with a tempo map, and a MusicXML score
Pull request #1 Branch
Notation and tablature

Read the part while the song plays

A Notation drawer shows the transcribed stems as engraved music, drawn by a vendored copy of alphaTab. StemDeck's own player stays the sound source. The cursor follows it at half speed, through loops and after a seek, and clicking a bar moves the song there.

  • Guitar, bassStaff, tab or both. A fingering step picks a playable string and fret for every note from your tuning and capo, and says how many notes it had to leave off.
  • FollowThe score scrolls with the song. Turn it off to look ahead, and the choice is saved for that song.
  • Per songWhich stems are shown, zoom, note resolution, tuning and capo
  • ExportsMIDI, MusicXML with a real tab staff, and Guitar Pro (.gp) for tab editors
Pull request #2 Branch
Visualizers · Labs

Six ways to watch a mix

The Visualizer opens from the sidebar over the studio. It follows the player and the mixer, so a muted stem goes dark and a soloed stem stands alone. It makes no sound and changes nothing.

Nobody knows yet which picture is worth keeping, so each one is a single folder. One command removes a picture, or the whole view, and a test checks that nothing is left behind.

Pull request #3 Branch

Note terrain

A landscape of blocks. Time runs into the screen, pitch runs across, and height shows how hard each note was played.

Slit-scan score

The whole song as one strip, a band per stem. Click anywhere to jump there.

Note galaxy

One spiral arm per stem. Every note fires particles at a distance set by its pitch.

Lit stage

A small stage whose keys, frets and drums light up note by note.

Song planet

The whole song wrapped once around a small world. Playback walks around it.

Drive the song

A road at the song's tempo. Drum hits are bumps and sections are the scenery.

Already in StemDeck

Then sing it, or play it and film yourself

The notes are new. These two modes were already there, and they are why the stems matter.

Karaoke

The lyrics fill the window

Words light up one by one as the song plays, with the lead voice taken out. Turn the backing vocals on or off, and drag songs from your library into a queue.

Performance

Take your part out and play it yourself

Tell StemDeck what you play with I play, and that stem leaves the backing track you hear. Film yourself playing along, review the take in step with the song, trim it, and export an MP4.

Try it

Run a branch from the fork

These are proposals on a fork, not a StemDeck release. StemDeck takes outside changes as issues that link a fork branch, so the pull requests live on natea/stemdeck for review.

Transcription downloads its models on first use, about 700 MB. It is not offered on Intel Macs, which are held at an older torch.

# the notation branch includes transcription
git clone https://github.com/natea/stemdeck
cd stemdeck
git switch alphatab-notation   # or stem-visualizers

uv sync
uv run uvicorn app.main:app --port 8000
# then open http://127.0.0.1:8000
